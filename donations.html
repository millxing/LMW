Donations

Thank you for any donations you may have previously made to Longwood Mall. Since the mid-1980s, Longwood Mall has been preserved only through a private-public partnership between Brookline residents and Brookline's Parks department.

We need to raise private funds to ensure that the trees of Longwood Mall are prioritized by the Town when it comes to tree cultivation, medical treatments, and new tree plantings.

If you would like to make a donation to Friends of Longwood Mall (100% of donations go to preserving the park), please make a check out to:

Town of Brookline: Trust for Longwood Mall

and mail it to:

Milee Pradhan Director of Parks and Open Space Town Hall – 4th Floor 333 Washington Street Brookline, MA 02445

If you do make a donation, please email me, so we can track donations made for Longwood Mall. Also, feel free to contact me if you would like to use Donor Advised Funds or have any other questions.
