Ways to Support Longwood Mall

Please consider making a Donation.

Please email Friends of Longwood Mall if you would be interested in being contacted the next time we do fundraising. 100% of donations go to planting new trees and caring for the existing trees. Donor-advised funds can be used.

People often leave trash on Longwood Mall. If you see garbage on the mall, please grab it and dispose of it.

If you see anyone sitting on tree trunks or branches (even little kids), please gently tell them that this is very damaging to the trees.
