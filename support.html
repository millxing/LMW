Get involved

Support Longwood Mall

The grove survives because neighbors care for it. Here is how you can help, whether you have five minutes or want to make a gift.

How to donateContact the Friends
01

Make a donation

Gifts go to a Town of Brookline trust fund for Longwood Mall. 100% of donations go to planting new trees and caring for the existing ones. Donor-advised funds are welcome.

How to donate
02

Lend a hand in the park

If you see litter, please pick it up. If you see anyone sitting on trunks or branches, even little kids, gently let them know it damages the trees.

Read the park rules
03

Stay in touch

Email the Friends of Longwood Mall to hear about the next fundraiser or event, or follow the park on Instagram.

Longwood MallHistoric public park · Brookline, MA

A historic public park in Brookline, Massachusetts, home to what is generally considered the oldest grove of European Beech trees in North America. Cared for by the Town of Brookline and the volunteers of Friends of Longwood Mall.
