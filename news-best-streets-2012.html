Beech Road tops the Boston Globe's 20 best streets

Posted by Friends of Longwood Mall

In 2012, Boston Globe Magazine did a story on the 20 best streets in the Boston metro area. At the top of the list was Beech Road in Brookline. While Longwood Mall is not directly mentioned, it's clear that the trees were a major factor in their assessment of the street.

Longwood MallHistoric public park · Brookline, MA

A historic public park in Brookline, Massachusetts, home to what is generally considered the oldest grove of European Beech trees in North America. Cared for by the Town of Brookline and the volunteers of Friends of Longwood Mall.
