Two new European Beeches planted along Chatham Street

Posted by Friends of Longwood Mall

In the first week of June, two young Fagus sylvatica (European Beeches) were planted along Chatham Street between Hawes Street and Borland Street.

Planting some young European Beech trees is part of the diversification plan for Longwood Mall. Most of the existing European Beeches are already mature, and many are very old. Adding a younger generation now gives the park a better chance of still having large, mature European Beeches in the future.

The broader plan also includes planting other species, so Longwood Mall is not dependent on a single kind of tree. These two new European Beeches help preserve the historic character of the park while the overall tree canopy becomes more age-diverse and species-diverse.
