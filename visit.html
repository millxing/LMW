Visit Longwood Mall

A quiet, open lawn under some of the oldest European Beech trees in North America. Free and open to everyone, every day from dawn to dusk.

Getting there

By public transit

Longwood Mall is one block from the Hawes Street stop on the MBTA Green Line C branch, about a five-minute walk. From the stop, walk south on Hawes Street to Beech Road.

By car

There is no parking lot. Parking is not allowed on Beech Road or Chatham Street on weekdays before 11 a.m. Street parking on the surrounding residential streets is limited and follows posted Brookline rules, including the town's overnight parking ban.

On foot or by bike

The park sits in a residential neighborhood near the Longwood Medical Area and Coolidge Corner. Please walk bikes on the lawn.

Protect the trees

Park rules

Longwood Mall is reserved for quiet enjoyment. These posted rules exist to protect fragile, very old trees and their shallow roots.

  • No dogs, including on leash
  • No climbing or sitting on trunks and branches
  • No ball playing
  • No smoking
  • Please carry out any trash or recycling
