June 8 event celebrates Longwood Mall's community and future

Posted by Friends of Longwood Mall

More than 110 people from across Brookline gathered under the European Beech trees on the evening of June 8, 2026, and heard some very good news. Brookline's Tree Warden and Urban Forestry Manager, Jack Schleifer, explained that the Beech Leaf Disease treatments appear to be working: there is little visible evidence of BLD in Longwood Mall today.

Jack also put the condition of the trees in context. Some of the older beeches are not in perfect health, but that does not necessarily mean BLD is taking hold. Many are very old, and some are dealing with age-related decline or other diseases that have affected beeches for years. The plan is to keep monitoring carefully and to pursue maintenance that may help the more stressed trees.

The event was sponsored by the Brookline GreenSpace Alliance (BGSA) and Friends of Longwood Mall. BGSA president Sean Lynn Jones opened the evening, Rob Schoen of Friends of Longwood Mall spoke about the park's long history, and Ellen Golde, one of the group's founders, shared the story of its origins in the early 1980s and its decades of work to protect the park.

After questions, Jack led a guided tour of the park. The evening ended with cookies, conversation, and Jack planting two tiny Japanese Beech saplings.

Thank you to everyone who came out. The turnout, the questions, and the energy around the park all give real reason to be optimistic about Longwood Mall's future.
