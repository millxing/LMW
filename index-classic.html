Longwood Mall

Welcome to the Longwood Mall website. This site contains information about Longwood Mall, a historic linear park in Brookline, Massachusetts. The park is known for its European Beech trees, many of which were planted by David Sears in the late 1840s. It is generally considered to be the oldest grove of European Beech trees in North America.

“

The finest exotic trees which have been planted in greater Boston and probably the finest grove of European Beech in the United States.

Charles Sprague Sargent, Arnold Arboretum

Beech Leaf Disease

Context For The
Biggest Current Threat

This section explains what BLD is, links to reliable outside resources, and points visitors toward treatment notes and public discussions.

Cause

BLD is associated with a foliar nematode, Litylenchus crenatae mccannii, that feeds within beech leaves and disrupts healthy growth.

Symptoms

Typical symptoms include dark interveinal striping, thinning canopy, reduced new growth, and broader decline, especially in younger trees.

Range

First identified in Ohio in 2012, BLD has spread widely across the Northeast, including Massachusetts.

Important context

This site's treatment notes currently run through December 2024

Support Can Mean
Money, Time, Or Care

Not everyone helps in the same way. The support page makes room for donations, partnership, everyday stewardship, and contact points.

Please email Friends of Longwood Mall if you would be interested in being contacted the next time we do fundraising. 100% of donations go to planting new trees and caring for the existing trees. Donor-advised funds can be used.

People often leave trash on Longwood Mall. If you see garbage on the mall, please grab it and dispose of it.

If you see anyone sitting on tree trunks or branches (even little kids), please gently tell them that this is very damaging to the trees.
