Longwood Mall

A public park and home to what is generally considered the oldest grove of European Beech trees in North America, many planted by David Sears in the late 1840s.

Beech Leaf Disease

Treatment appears to be working

Beech Leaf Disease has killed beech trees across the Northeast. Since 2022 the grove has received regular treatment, and in June 2026 Brookline's Tree Warden reported little visible evidence of the disease at Longwood Mall.
